An honest word first. The website’s technical side is strong, but nobody can promise to “beat” an established brand such as Ismail Farid. Google ranks mostly on trust built over time: how many respected sites link to you, how long the brand has existed, how many people search for it by name, and reviews. A new site cannot buy that in a month. What we can do is remove every technical obstacle, give Google and AI assistants clean facts, and choose battles we can win first (section 5).

5. A realistic plan against big brands

  1. Win your own name first. Searching “Nure Asmir” should show the shop first (usually within weeks of indexing). Verify Search Console, submit the sitemap, add Google Business Profile.
  2. Free Google Shopping listings. Connect the product feed to Merchant Center; products can show in the Shopping tab without ads once real products are live.
  3. Specific searches, not general ones. “Ismail Farid” will win “men’s shalwar kameez Pakistan” for a long time. We can compete on narrower phrases: fabric, occasion, city (“wash and wear kameez Lahore”, “Eid kurta for men”), and our own collections.
  4. Reviews and photos from real customers (then add star markup).
  5. Links: fashion bloggers, local press, wedding and Eid features, supplier and partner sites.
  6. Measure monthly in Search Console: impressions, clicks, average position per phrase. Judge progress after 3 months, not 3 days.
Timing the owner should expect: Google needs days to weeks to first list a new page and can take up to a month; moving up the rankings takes months. AI assistants update even more slowly and unpredictably.
